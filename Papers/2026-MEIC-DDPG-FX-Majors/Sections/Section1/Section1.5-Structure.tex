\subsection{Structure}

The rest of this document is organised as follows. Section~\ref{Section:Background} covers the material needed to follow the text: how the foreign exchange market works, how technical analysis summarises a price series, and how neural networks and reinforcement learning combine into the methods used here, ending with DDPG. Section~\ref{Section:RelatedWork} reviews the published applications of these methods to trading, grouped by market and by algorithm, and states what the existing work leaves open. Section~\ref{Section:Implementation} describes the system that was built: the framework and its connection to cTrader, the backtesting engine and its cost model, and the design of the observation, the action and the reward. Section~\ref{Section:ExperimentalResults} reports the evaluation protocol, the results for each of the seven pairs, their robustness and the attribution of the returns. Section~\ref{Section:Conclusion} summarises what was achieved, states the limitations of the study and describes the work that follows from it.
